# Astra rules and research decisions

Astra uses the [original Quadradius directions](https://quadradius.ddns.net/directions.html), with the local `research/quadradius/game_details/powers.md` as a secondary reference. The original named list contains **86 collectible powers**, including member powers. Cancel Multiply is a cancellation control, not a collectible. The research summary's count of 87 includes that control.

The arena is 10 columns by 8 rows, with heights from -4 to +4. Powers belong to individual pieces. Activating a power spends one copy before its effect; effects do not end the turn. Movement normally ends the turn. Five initial orbs and five new orbs every 14 completed turns are Astra tuning. All collectible types have equal spawn probability. Inventory reaching ten copies of a type destroys its piece and tile.

The following decisions resolve details where the directions are incomplete:

- Area powers operate around the activating piece. Base radial powers affect the eight surrounding cells; Scramble, Swap and Kamikaze also include the caster. Plateau and Moat include its tile. Row and column powers include the caster's band. Grow extends Chebyshev radius from 1 to 4 and band half-width from 0 to 3; no wrapping of power areas. Refurb, Bankrupt and Dredge also use this common geometry although they are absent from the original Grow examples.
- Raise and Lower alter the caster's tile by one and reject at the height limit. Wall/Trench set the band to maximum/minimum; Moat sets its center to maximum and neighbors to minimum. Terrain powers do not repair holes except Refurb. Refurb resets height, repairs holes and removes tile devices, while retaining existing orbs.
- Bombs produce eight impacts per activation, multiplied by `1 + Grow`. Each impact samples an intact tile independently, so repeat hits are possible. Smart Bombs exclude tiles occupied by allies. Impacts lower one level, making a hole only when the tile was already at minimum. Snake Tunneling walks 12 times `1 + Grow` steps, starting beneath the caster, choosing uniformly among orthogonal in-bounds neighbors; it raises intact tiles and kills enemies. These numerical budgets are Astra tuning: the directions do not specify exact values.
- Multiply chooses an empty, intact adjacent tile in any of eight directions, ignoring elevation. Relocate selects an empty intact tile without an orb. Scramble redistributes all affected pieces without replacement over all intact tiles in its footprint, including previously empty cells. Displacement triggers landing effects and Tripwire.
- Teach/Learn copy inventories; Pilfer/Beneficiary transfer them. Installed abilities are not inventory. The original directions explicitly prohibit doubling other 2x copies; Astra follows this even though the secondary research says all powers.
- Switcheroo and Centerpult install movement abilities. The second Switcheroo stage exchanges positions with enemies instead of capturing them. The second Scavenger stage permits allied captures. Further stage upgrades reject. Move Again installs an extra move on the caster; after that caster moves the same player can move any piece.
- Parasite marks infected pieces with the infecting owner. Orb collection distributes one copy to every living friendly parasite receiver and Network Bridge, without recursively triggering another network. Scavenger shares captured inventory with allied scavengers and bridges. Power Plant joins the installer to that owner's plant network and marks the current tile; orbs appearing on empty plant tiles distribute to plant receivers and bridges. Inhibit prevents direct orb collection, not inventory transfers. Tile devices store one owner; later installations replace prior ownership.
- Purify removes Tripwire, Inhibit, Parasite infection and Spyware from allies, and beneficial flags from enemies. It leaves inventories unchanged. Bankrupt removes an entering enemy's inventory and beneficial flags. Jump Proof blocks occupation captures, not powers. Invisible persists; visibility is a presentation/information rule rather than invulnerability.

All authoritative random effects use the serialized state RNG. No scene nodes enter core state. Rules performs cloning, inventory consumption, landing effects, overheat, victory and turn completion; Powers validates and mutates only the working state. Power previews use the same area geometry as execution. Multiplayer information filtering and undisclosed historical edge cases are not claims of exact original-server parity.
